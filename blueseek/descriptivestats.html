Descriptive Statistics Explained

Central Tendency

  • 1
    Mean:

    The average value of the dataset

  • 2
    Median:

    The middle value when data is ordered

  • 3
    Mode:

    The most frequently occurring value

Dispersion

  • 1
    Variance:

    Measures how far each number is from the mean

  • 2
    Standard Deviation:

    Square root of variance, shows spread

  • 3
    IQR:

    Interquartile range (middle 50% of data)
